# ProSpacer Community Standards

ProSpacer is created and maintained by Profexor. Collaboration should be technically rigorous, respectful, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly and critique work, not people.
- Give reproduction steps, evidence, and constraints when raising problems.
- Welcome different experience levels and explain project-specific assumptions.
- Respect privacy, security reports, and embargoed vulnerabilities.
- Credit contributions accurately without misrepresenting project ownership.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person’s private information.
- Deliberately disruptive reviews, spam, or off-topic promotion.
- Misrepresenting authority, authorship, affiliation, or endorsement.
- Removing or falsifying copyright, license, or trademark notices.
- Sharing exploitable security details outside the private reporting process.

## Enforcement

Profexor may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restriction from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

When reviewing a report, the maintainer will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected participants.
