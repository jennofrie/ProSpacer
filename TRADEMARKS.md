These uses must not imply endorsement and should use only as much branding as necessary for identification.

## Forks and modifications

Forks may exercise the permissions granted by the MIT License. Publicly distributed forks should:

1. preserve the MIT copyright and permission notice;
2. choose a distinct product name and visual identity unless written permission is granted;
3. state that the fork is unofficial;
4. avoid suggesting endorsement by Profexor.

The repository includes repeated Markdown notices, ownership rules, and an automated integrity check. These controls detect accidental removal in normal development workflows; they do not alter runtime behavior or replace legal enforcement.
